For the grown-ups

Your information.

When you sign up, we save your email address, the time of your signup, and a record that you agreed to receive launch updates from The Hanukkah Fairy.

What the list is for

Book launch updates, news about the characters, and future plush announcements. Signing up does not place an order or reserve a product.

Where your signup goes

Signups are stored in a private CSV file using Vercel, our website and storage provider. The list is not publicly downloadable from this website.

For adults

This signup is for parents and other grown-ups. Please use your own email address. We don’t ask for children’s names, ages, or email addresses.

Changing your mind

To request removal before launch emails begin, contact @thehanukkahfairy on Instagram. You can ask to leave the list at any time.
